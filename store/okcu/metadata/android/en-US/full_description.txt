Pull back, let go and knock wobbly archers off their floating islands, now in 3D.
Fight through six worlds and six bosses, unlock six weapons and level them up with shards from chests.

Noodle Archers is a bright, bouncy archery game in side-view 3D with ragdoll physics. Your first arrow is in the air within seconds: drag anywhere to aim, watch the dotted arc, release to shoot.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Drag back to set angle and power, then let go. No buttons to learn.
• Arrows really fly: they arc, stick where they land and shove what they hit.
• Where you hit matters: the head knocks an archer out, a leg hit can topple them off the ledge.
• Rival archers shoot back, and they aim well. Shoot the apples that drift by to heal.
• Every archer is a 3D character on a ragdoll: a hit shoves them, a knockout sends them flying.

SIX CHAPTERS
• Sunny Meadow, Frozen Peaks, Sunset Dunes, Lantern Night, Ember Caldera and Sky Citadel, seven stages each, then endless waves.
• An elite with loot in the middle of every chapter and a boss at the end: the Bramble Baron, the Frost Giant, the Sand Sultan, the Night Warden, the Cinder King and the Storm Caller. Each one changes tactics at half health.
• A bonus round after every chapter: target gallery, apple rain or defend the ledge, with medals.
• Sliding ledges, ledges hanging on ropes you can cut, rocks to drop, explosive barrels and wind you have to aim into.
• New rivals: healers, archers behind cover walls, arrow-catchers and lantern knights.

SIX WEAPONS
• Bow, crossbow, spear, boomerang, sling and firework tube, each with its own flight: flat crossbow bolts, spears that pierce, a boomerang that hits on the way back, pebbles that bounce and fireworks that burst over a crowd.
• Beat a chapter's boss to unlock its weapon, or collect the sling from chest shards.
• Level every weapon from 1 to 20 with shards and coins. Each level adds damage, and some add a perk.

CHESTS
• A wooden chest to open every 4 hours; watch an optional ad to make it silver.
• Chests from runs, bosses and the daily challenge, and chests you can buy with coins.
• Every chest shows its odds on screen, exactly as they are rolled.

BIG MOMENTS
• Headshot chains, multi-kills, knockout streaks and critical hits, each with its own callout.
• Fill the focus meter and tap the eye for a powered-up shot.
• Pick one of three trick cards after every stage: 18 tricks, from split shot and fire arrows to seeker arrows and chain sparks, and six combos that unlock when two tricks meet.

YOUR PROGRESS
• Climb a personal ladder of 14 ranks, from Recruit to Eternal Archer.
• A records board of your own bests, with dates, and achievements that pay coins.
• A daily challenge with a twist and a goal, and a streak for coming back.
• Four permanent upgrades. Health upgrades suit your archer up in armour.
• A tabbed main menu: Shop, Weapons, Home, Ranks and Events. Start any chapter you have reached from Home.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Plays offline. No account needed.
• No real-money purchases: everything is earned with coins. Ads are optional and only give a reward when you choose to watch one.
• Available in English and Turkish.
