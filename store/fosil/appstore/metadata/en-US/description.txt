Every fossil you dig up comes stamped with its true odds, from 1 in 2 to 1 in 1,000,000.
Swing your pickaxe through thirteen layers of Earth's past, fill your museum and chase the one-in-a-million find.

Fossil Odds is a quick, satisfying digging game about luck you can actually see. The number printed on a find is the real chance of that fossil in its layer, and the luck it was rolled at is always shown with it.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Tap the rock: your pickaxe swings, chips fly and the block splits into tumbling chunks.
• Each broken block rolls one fossil from that layer, and every fossil you own adds to your dig power.
• Keep tapping in rhythm for a combo that raises the luck of your taps up to ×2. Fill the fever meter for eight seconds of double damage and double dust.
• Break blocks quickly one after another for a chain bonus. Catch a glint for one roll at 10× luck.
• Your helper digs beside you and gets better gear as you level it up.
• The rarer the find, the bigger the reveal: common finds pop out, rare ones flip in on a card, the rarest stop time.

THIRTEEN LAYERS, EACH WITH A TWIST
• Topsoil, Ice Age, Cretaceous, Deep Sea, Coal Swamp, Amber Forest, Salt Caves, Crystal Geode, Magma Vents, First Seas, Abyssal Trench, Star Crater and Time Rift, each with its own scenery, rock and 12 fossils.
• Hard veins you hold and release to smash, unstable blocks that pay a bonus roll if you break them in time, twin blocks with two fossils, relic fragments, cave-ins full of dust, a boss block that rolls at triple luck, a pressure gauge in the trench, meteor star cores and echoes of older layers in the rift.
• Golden blocks turn up everywhere once you are going: two fossils and five times the dust.

FAIR LUCK
• Every probability is printed, including the boss rolls.
• Lucky streak: after 150 finds in a row without a 1 in 100 or rarer, the next find is guaranteed to be one, drawn in exactly the printed proportions. The rule is shown in the game.
• Nothing about luck can be bought.

COLLECT AND GROW
• 156 fossils in seven rarity tiers, from Common to Singular.
• Three copies fuse into bronze, silver, then gold, each worth more dig power. Complete a layer's set for a permanent bonus.
• Walk your museum: shelves for every layer, a spotlight on your rarest find and printed odds under every silhouette you have not found yet.
• Upgrade your pickaxe (five looks, from wood to crystal), lucky charm, helper and night shift.

YOUR OWN PROGRESS
• Expeditions: once you reach First Seas, start again from Topsoil in a deeper stratum. You keep your museum, set bonuses, relics, records and goals, and gain dig power and dust for good.
• Climb 15 ranks, from Pebble Picker to Legend of the Layers. The ladder counts only your own progress.
• A records board of your rarest finds and fastest sets, 38 goals that pay dust, and a daily dig with a streak.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Plays offline. No account needed. Your helper keeps digging for a while when you are away.
• Optional online leaderboards with Game Center for players aged 13 and over.
• Available in English and Turkish.
