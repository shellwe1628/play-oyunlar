Six weapons: bow, crossbow, spear, boomerang, sling and firework tube, each with its own flight. Beat bosses to unlock them, then level them 1 to 20 with shards from chests. A new tabbed menu: Shop, Weapons, Home, Ranks and Events. The forge is retired: your bow keeps its strength as a weapon level, and a gold welcome chest waits for you. Armoured enemies, stage modifiers and wider fields. Optional rewarded ads (AD) give an extra card, a revive, double coins, a silver chest or coins.
