Hold to veer right, let go to veer left: one thumb steers a spark up a glowing neon shaft.
Climb fifty levels across five worlds, face a boss at the end of each, and leave a trail that looks like nothing else.

Sparkrise is a fast one-touch reflex game. The whole screen is the button: the spark always climbs, and its direction changes the instant your finger goes down or comes up. No drifting, no gravity, just the line you draw.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Hold anywhere to fly up and right, let go to fly up and left. That is the whole control.
• Walls, spiked blocks and moving bars close in. One touch and the spark shatters.
• A tap after a crash restarts the level at once. No menus, no waiting.
• Every run opens slowly in a wide chamber, so you can find the rhythm before it gets tight.
• Gates change the rules on the way: faster, slower, smaller, bigger, or flipped controls.

FIVE WORLDS, FIFTY LEVELS
• Ignition, Prism Works, Storm Spire, Void Gate and Vortex, each level with its own animated backdrop that pumps with the beat.
• New tricks arrive one at a time: split paths, lasers that blink on the beat, spinners, wind that bends your line, sliding walls, dark sections lit only by your spark, portals and a mirror twin that has to survive too.
• Every world ends in a boss finale: a giant eye watches from the backdrop while you crack its health bar section by section.
• Three stars per level: finish it, take all three gems in one run, and finish without a near miss. Stars open the next world.

WORLD 5: VORTEX
• The view itself twists: dive turns the shaft upside down, sideways turns the climb into a flight to the right, and the world leans, sways or zooms out and in.
• Every twist is announced before it starts, with an arrow that shows where holding will take you.
• Vortex opens after level 40 once you have 80 stars.

TRAILS
• 23 trails from common to mythic, from a simple ribbon to sparks, lightning, auroras, galaxies and colour-shifting top tiers.
• Five of them can only be earned: by rank, by a daily streak, by clean runs and by clearing the last two worlds.
• Eight spark shapes. In the shop you can watch every trail live, and try one for a run before you spend gems on it. Gems are earned in play; trails are purely cosmetic.

YOUR PROGRESS
• Climb a personal ladder of 12 ranks, from Ember to Supernova.
• A records board with your best on every level and the date, your endless and dive bests and your daily history.
• 23 feats that pay gems.
• The flow meter fills as you take gems and fly clean sections: more music layers, a brighter world and a bigger trail.

MORE MODES
• Endless: the shaft never ends and the speed creeps up. How high can you climb?
• Dive: an endless shaft seen upside down, falling instead of climbing.
• Endless and Dive open after level 3.
• Daily shaft: a new shaft every day, with a streak reward for coming back.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Plays offline. No account needed.
• Optional rewarded ads, only when you choose them: the buttons marked AD (continue after a crash, double gems after a finish).
• Optional online ranking with Game Center.
• Available in English and Turkish.
