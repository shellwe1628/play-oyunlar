Hold to veer right, let go to veer left: one thumb steers a spark up a glowing neon shaft.
Thread the zigzag, grab the gems, never touch the walls. Crash, tap, and you are flying again.

Sparkrise is a fast one-touch reflex game. The whole screen is the button: the spark always climbs, and its direction changes the instant your finger goes down or comes up. No drifting, no gravity, just the line you draw.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Hold anywhere to fly up and right, let go to fly up and left. That is the whole control.
• Walls, spiked blocks and moving bars close in. One touch and the spark shatters.
• A tap after a crash restarts the level at once. No menus, no waiting.
• Every run opens slowly in a wide chamber, so you can find the rhythm before it gets tight.
• Your best percentage is kept, so every attempt shows how much further you got.

GATES THAT CHANGE THE RULES
• FAST and SLOW gates change your speed until a 1× gate sets it back.
• MINI shrinks your spark to slip through tighter gaps; BIG brings it back.
• FLIP swaps your controls (holding now goes left!) until the mint NORMAL gate puts them back. A "FLIPPED" tag rides under the spark the whole time.

LEVELS AND MODES
• Ten hand-built levels, each in its own colours, each one harder than the last.
• Every level hides three gems on the riskier lines.
• Endless mode (opens after level 3): the shaft never ends and the speed slowly creeps up. How high can you climb?
• Daily shaft: one new shaft every day, with your best height for the day and your streak.

SKINS
• Spend gems on new spark shapes and trail colours: eight of each. Purely cosmetic.

FEEL
• A driving beat, made in code, keeps time with your speed. Skim a wall and it throws sparks.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Plays offline. No account needed.
• Available in English and Turkish.
