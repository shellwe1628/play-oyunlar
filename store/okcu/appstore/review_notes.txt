Thank you for reviewing Noodle Archers.

ACCOUNT, SERVER, CONTENT
- No account, no login and no sign-in is needed to play. There is no server of ours and no remote configuration: the whole game (3D, drawn with WebGL) is bundled in the app and works offline. Progress is saved on the device only.
- Every player gets the same features. Nothing is unlocked, switched on or changed remotely; chapters, weapons and modes open only through play on the device.
- No real-money purchases. Coins and weapon shards are earned in play; the Shop and the chests use coins only. Every chest shows its odds on screen, exactly as they are rolled.
- This build removes the developer test code (test hooks and URL switches) that earlier builds still contained.
- Violence is cartoon style: wobbly ragdoll archers are knocked off floating islands, with no blood.

CONTROLS
- Drag anywhere to aim (the dotted arc shows the shot), release to shoot.

HOW TO REACH EVERY MODE AND SCREEN
- The app opens on the Home tab of the main menu. Tabs along the bottom: Shop, Weapons, Home, Ranks, Events. Home has Play, the chapter card (arrows to pick any chapter already reached), the free chest button, the daily button and the Privacy link.
- Chapters: 6 chapters of 7 stages; each ends with a boss. A chapter becomes selectable on Home once a run has reached it.
- Weapons tab: six weapons. The bow is owned from the start; the crossbow, spear, boomerang, firework tube and sling unlock by beating chapter bosses (the sling can also come from chest shards). Weapons are levelled with shards and coins.
- Camera angles: in play, the camera button at the top left switches between Side, Angled and Low.
- Events tab: the daily challenge, and the two modes played in Sunset Canyon. Blitz (60 seconds against endless archers) opens after the first run (as soon as one arrow has been fired in a run). Boss Rush (all six bosses back to back) opens once the chapter 1 boss has been beaten. Until then their cards show a lock with this condition.
- Ranks tab: the rank ladder, records, achievements and the online button.

DATE AND TIME BASED CONTENT
- Free chest: one wooden chest every 4 hours (the first one is ready at once), from the chest button on Home.
- Shop: the shard offers change and the purchase limits reset each calendar day (local midnight on the device); the "AD: +coins" button can be used at most 3 times a day.
- Daily challenge (Events tab): a new challenge each calendar day (local midnight), with a streak; meeting its goal gives a gold chest. Mode runs (Blitz, Boss Rush) give a run chest for the first 3 mode runs of each day.

ADS
- Rewarded ads (Google AdMob) are shown only when the player taps a button marked with the "AD" label: one more card on the card pick; revive and double coins on the result screen; turning the free wooden chest into a silver one; extra coins in the Shop (at most 3 a day). All are optional; the game never requires them. There are no other ads.
- Every ad request is child-directed and non-personalised. There is no tracking, the IDFA is not read and there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

ONLINE RANKING (OPTIONAL)
- Game Center only: Ranks tab > the globe button (top right). There is no age question on iPhone. Game Center is not started before the player taps Sign in there; Apple's own sign-in appears if the device is not signed in. Scores go only to Game Center. Nothing else in the game needs a sign-in.

PRIVACY
- The privacy policy is linked from the main menu (Privacy, on the Home tab) and from the online panel.
