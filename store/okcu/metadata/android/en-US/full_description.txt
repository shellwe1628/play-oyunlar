Pull back, let go and knock wobbly noodle archers off their floating ledges.
Land a headshot for a slow-motion knockout, then pick a new trick and take on the next wave.

Noodle Archers is a bright, bouncy archery game with ragdoll physics. Your first arrow is in the air within seconds: drag anywhere to aim, watch the dotted arc, release to shoot.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Drag back to set angle and power, then let go. No buttons to learn.
• Arrows really fly: they arc, stick where they land and shove what they hit.
• Where you hit matters: the head knocks an archer out, a leg hit can topple them off the ledge.
• Rival archers shoot back, so keep an eye on your health bar. Shoot the apples that drift by to heal.
• Clear every archer to finish the wave. Runs are short, and a new one is a tap away.

PICK A TRICK
• After every wave choose one of three trick cards: split shot, double shot, fire arrows, bouncing and piercing arrows, quicker draw, a protective bubble and more.
• Tricks stack and combine, so a good run gets wildly stronger as it goes.

RIVALS
• Plain archers, shield bearers, jumpers that hop between ledges and bombers whose bombs you can shoot out of the air.
• Every fifth wave an armoured boss shows up: aim for the glowing head.
• The sky changes colour every five waves.

BETWEEN RUNS
• Spend the coins you earn on five permanent upgrades.
• Reach new best waves to unlock hats and scarf colours (six looks in all).
• Try the daily run: the same archers for that day's date, without upgrades.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Plays offline. No account needed.
• Available in English and Turkish.
