Every fossil you dig up comes stamped with its true odds, from 1 in 2 to 1 in 1,000,000.
Tap the rock, fill your museum and chase the one-in-a-million find.

Fossil Odds is a calm, quick digging game about luck you can actually see. The number printed on a find is the real chance of that fossil in its layer, and your current luck multiplier is always on screen.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Tap the rock to dig. Each broken block rolls one fossil from that layer.
• Every fossil you own adds to your dig power, so each find makes the next block break faster.
• Keep tapping in rhythm to build a combo that raises the luck of your taps up to ×2.
• Catch a glint before it fades for one roll at 10× luck.
• The rarer the find, the bigger the reveal: common finds pop out, rare ones flip in on a card, and the rarest stop time.

COLLECT AND GROW
• 48 fossils across four ages: Topsoil, Ice Age, Cretaceous and Deep Sea.
• Seven rarity tiers, from Common to Singular.
• Three copies of a fossil fuse into the next grade: bronze, silver, then gold, each worth more dig power.
• Complete a layer's set for a permanent bonus.
• Your museum shows every fossil you have found, and a silhouette with printed odds for every one you have not.

UPGRADES
• Pick: more dig power with every tap.
• Lucky charm: better odds on rare finds.
• Assistant: digs on its own, even while you are away (at a lower rate, for a limited time).
• Night shift: lets the assistant keep digging longer while you are away.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Plays offline. No account needed.
• Available in English and Turkish.
